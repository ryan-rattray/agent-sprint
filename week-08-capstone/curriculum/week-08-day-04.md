Read through what you've built once, end to end, like a stranger. Note
what's missing, what's confusing, what you'd demo first. That's it. The
sprint has stairs, not deadlines, and today is a landing, not a climb.

3. **Rest.** The week's done criteria don't need today.

refactor, stop. Write it down, park it, close the laptop. The day stays
light so Friday stays sharp.
