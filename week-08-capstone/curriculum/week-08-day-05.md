# Week 8, Day 5 — Numbers and Write-up (Friday, Nov 27)

## The idea

The capstone's product was never just the agent; it's the evidence. Today
you assemble it: the final measurements (costs for C1, correct-call rates
for C2, the demo run for the default), the one-page write-up (what worked,
what failed, what you'd do with four more weeks), and the public repo with
a README that explains the architecture in 30 seconds.

## What you'll build

Final numbers, the write-up, the repo README.

## Steps

1. **Run the final measurements.** Record the numbers, whatever they are.
   Negative results are results.
2. **Type the one-page write-up.** What worked, what failed, what you'd do
   with four more weeks. Hook first, specific nouns, tight sentences.
3. **Write the README.** Architecture in 30 seconds, how to run the demo,
   what the numbers mean.
4. **Commit.** `week-08 day-05: capstone numbers, write-up, and README`.
   Breakage log: the sprint's last entry.

## Done criteria (binary)

- [ ] A demo-able end-to-end run exists and you've watched it work.
- [ ] The public repo has a README explaining the architecture in 30
      seconds.
- [ ] A one-page write-up exists: what worked, what failed, four more
      weeks.

## What this unlocks

- **Weekend:** final packaging and the retrospective. Then the curriculum
  ends Sunday, and the check-ins stop with it.

## Likely snag

**The numbers disappoint.** Then the write-up is about why, with evidence.
"The judge wasn't worth the cost" is a finding, not a failure. The frame
was always the measurement, not the agent.

## When you're done

Reply "Day 5 done". Weekend sheet: packaging and retrospective.
